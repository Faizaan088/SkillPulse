What is SkillPulse?

A different question from a conventional reporting dashboard

The Core SkillPulse Question

SkillPulse asks: 'What does the combined evidence imply for training-system decisions, where is the uncertainty, and why?'

Problem: Industry requirements evolve faster than training systems can adapt. Traditional courses continue teaching legacy skills while employers request newer competencies.

PS 134 represents a continuous alignment bridge between Industry, Training Institutes (ITIs/Polytechnics), Government Planners, and Candidates.

What SkillPulse is NOT

  • Not a standard job portal or recruitment site
  • Not a generic static LMIS report
  • Not a conversational chatbot guessing answers
  • Not an autonomous policymaker replacing human leadership
  • Not an unexplainable AI black-box system
