The 10-Layer Architecture

Evidence-to-action intelligence pipeline — deep dive into every layer

Home > Architecture Deep Dive

Layer 1: Evidence Ingestion

The foundation of SkillPulse. Ingests evidence from multiple sources while keeping them distinct.

Labour-Market Evidence

Job postings, public employment information, employer inputs, industry consultations.

Training Evidence

Training capacity, enrolment, completion, certification, available courses.

Outcome Evidence

Placement, tracer observations, retention, employer feedback.

Structural Evidence

Qualification frameworks, occupational classifications, sector trends, industrial clusters.

Key Principle

Evidence sources remain distinct so the system can explain what contributed to a signal.

Layer 2: Data Quality + Provenance

Observation Metadata

  • Source & Date
  • Geography
  • Evidence type & Freshness
  • Geographic precision & Confidence

Core Mechanics

  • Privacy/consent metadata for candidate information
  • Every recommendation exposes its supporting evidence through SHOW ME WHY
  • Audit trail for all transformations

Layer 3: Entity Resolution

Resolving Complex Data

Normalize inconsistent names: job titles, skill phrases, courses, districts.

Retain Raw Values

Alongside canonical interpretations

Stored Metadata

Confidence, mapping method, validation state

Human Validation

Aliases improve future interpretation

Concept of Reversible Intelligence

Layer 4: Multilingual Skill Intelligence

Supported Languages & Dialects

English, Hindi, Marathi, Hinglish, Maranglish, transliteration, informal technical language.

Skill Extraction & Routing

Extract candidate skills → map to canonical competencies → assign confidence.

Note: Route uncertain interpretations for review rather than silently forcing.

Layer 5: Market Intelligence

Demand States

Strong Moderate Weak Increasing Declining Emerging

Market Dynamics

Separate demand from market observability. Evidence fusion from multiple imperfect sources.

Evidence Bounds

Avoid claims of exact total job counts where evidence cannot support them.

Layer 6: Supply + Outcome Intelligence

Supply Differentiation

Distinguish nominal supply from effective supply.

States: Observed, Estimated, Unobserved.

Tracer Observations

3-, 6-, 12-month tracer observations. Track response selection risk.

Ranges & Sensitivity

Use ranges and sensitivity analysis for uncertainty.

Causality Principle

Never infer causality from training-to-employment leakage.

Layer 8: Labour-Market Geography

Geospatial Modeling

Model demand and supply across district boundaries.

Represent: employer clusters, training centres, districts, corridors.

Visibility Reality

Do not equate low online visibility with low actual demand.
