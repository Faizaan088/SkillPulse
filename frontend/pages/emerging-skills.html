The Skillness Gate

Distinguishing genuine technical competencies from job perks, shift conditions, and recruiter buzzwords

ACCEPTED

Genuine Competencies

Observable, teachable, and assessable operational capabilities acting on tools, materials, or data.

✓ EV Battery Thermal Management

✓ 5-Axis CNC Trajectory Tuning

✓ ROS2 Mobile Robot Calibration

✓ SCADA Modbus Interfacing

REJECTED AS CONDITION

Job Conditions & Perks

Operational terms of work that belong in compensation and logistic filters, NOT educational curricula.

✗ "Rotational Night Shift" (Work Condition)

✗ "Own 2-Wheeler Required" (Asset Condition)

✗ "Free Canteen & Transport" (Perk)

✗ "Urgent Joining Bonus" (Contract Terms)

REJECTED AS NOISE

Recruiter Clichés & Buzzwords

Unmeasurable subjective adjectives frequently inserted by HR personnel with zero vocational substance.

✗ "Dynamic Go-Getter" (Subjective Cliché)

✗ "Rockstar Work Ethic" (Unverifiable)

✗ "Hardworking & Obedient" (Attitudinal)

✗ "Out of the Box Thinker" (Noise)

Algorithmic Discrimination Mechanics

The Skillness Gate executes Dependency Tree Grammatical Parsing to verify whether candidate phrases function syntactically as direct objects of technical vocational action verbs (e.g. calibrate, program, solder, machine, inspect) versus adverbial clauses of condition or marketing superlatives. Non-competency phrases are diverted to compensation and location filters without polluting the skill taxonomy.

The 5-Pillar Evidence Hurdle

Preventing knee-jerk syllabus changes by enforcing rigorous statistical proof of sustained industrial demand

01

Repetition

Must appear in at least N ≥ 25 unique job listings across the targeted economic corridor.

Volume Proof
02

Independence

Must appear across at least ≥ 6 distinct, unaffiliated employer entities. Rules out single-firm staffing campaigns.

Syndicate Check
03

Persistence

Demand signal must be sustained across > 90 consecutive days. Eliminates transient seasonal spikes.

Time-Series Proof
04

Tech Relevance

Cosine similarity > 0.72 with technical domains in the canonical knowledge graph.

Vector Proximity
05

Qualification Gap

Cross-referenced against all 1,200+ active NSQF Qualification Packs to verify zero syllabus coverage.
