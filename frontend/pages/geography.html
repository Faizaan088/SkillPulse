Labour-Market Geography

Administrative geography is not automatically economic geography. SkillPulse models labor mobility, industrial corridors, and training capacity across district boundaries.

Economic Corridors vs. Administrative Districts

Conventional skill gap reports assume that workers live, train, and work within a single district boundary. In reality, modern industry functions along interconnected transit and logistics corridors. A trainee graduating from an ITI in rural Pune commutes into Chakan or Talegaon industrial parks; a polytechnic in Wardha feeds heavy manufacturing plants in Nagpur. SkillPulse unifies district data into true economic travel sheds.

Commute-Shed Optimization

Rather than opening duplicate specialized labs in neighboring talukas, SkillPulse computes travel times to suggest centralized high-end equipment hubs (e.g. advanced 5-axis CNC centres) accessible by shuttle transit.

Avoiding Rural Observability Blindspots

Low online job portal postings in semi-rural districts do NOT equate to zero demand. SkillPulse blends MSME cluster associations, industrial power consumption surges, and offline apprentice registrations to capture real economic gravity.
