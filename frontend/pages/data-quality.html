Data Quality & Provenance

Ensuring every insight is grounded in freshness, origin traceability, explicit confidence intervals, candidate consent privacy controls, and transparent failure-state management.

Six Data Quality Pillars

Every ingested observation is stamped with strict quality and provenance metadata

Freshness & Decay

Observations decay in weight over time. Daily job postings carry higher immediate freshness than quarterly employer surveys or annual census reports.

Source Provenance

Every point references its immutable source ID, ingestion method (API, survey, crawl), collection agency, and timestamp.

Geographic Precision

Distinguishes whether an observation applies to an exact industrial PIN code, a district administrative zone, or a state-wide economic corridor.

Confidence Scoring

Deterministic calculations score certainty from 0.0 to 1.0 based on cross-source agreement, sample size, and historical validation accuracy.

Privacy & Consent

Candidate-level tracking incorporates differential privacy, anonymized tracer records, and strict candidate consent governance under DPDP Act principles.

Immutable Audit Trail

Every normalization transform, entity alias substitution, and alignment gap score is logged in an append-only verifiable audit ledger.

Failure States & Implementation Honesty

SkillPulse refuses to silently hallucinate answers when data is missing or degraded. The system implements explicit failure state behaviors:

Source Unavailable

The system preserves the last recorded freshness timestamp and displays degraded confidence indicators rather than pretending the feed is live.

Low-Confidence Mapping

When an informal skill phrase has sub-threshold semantic matching confidence (< 0.65), it is routed to human curriculum review rather than forced into an erroneous classification.

Conflicting Sources

If online job postings show a 40% surge while employer surveys report neutral demand, both divergent views are presented side-by-side with an explanatory divergence flag.

Tracer Non-Response

Graduates who do not respond to follow-up surveys are explicitly labeled Unobserved. The system strictly adheres to the principle: Missing ≠ Failure.

Automated Validation Suite

Rigorous statistical checks verifying system integrity before recommendations deploy

Golden Entity Test

A curated benchmark of 2,500 multilingual job titles and vernacular skill phrases continuously evaluates entity normalization accuracy.

Fusion Divergence Check

Synthetic injection of conflicting data streams verifies that evidence fusion dampens erratic outliers and computes truthful confidence penalties.

Corridor Invariance Test

Ensures that industrial demand crossing district borders (e.g., Pune to Chakan) is properly modeled as unified economic geography.
