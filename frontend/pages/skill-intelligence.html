Canonical vs. Raw Values

RAW PROVENANCE

The Problem of Raw Expressions

Real-world vocational hiring does not follow textbook terminology. Employers and contractors write job notices using fragmented terminology, phonetic spellings, regional dialectical variations, and trade colloquialisms.

  • Spelling Variations: "Tork wielder", "Arck weldr", "Weldor MIG"
  • Regional Jargon: "Turnery master", "Setter-operator", "Helper-fitter"
  • Linguistic Mix: "सीएनसी ऑपरेटर", "CNC karigar", "Fitting ka mistri"
  • Risk of Naive Scraping: Creating 50 duplicate skills for the exact same physical competency, distorting demand signals by up to 400%.
CANONICAL MAPPING

The SkillPulse Canonical Resolution

Instead of discarding raw text or allowing unconstrained synonym explosion, SkillPulse applies a strict, dual-layer architecture: every mention resolves to an immutable Canonical Skill ID with mathematical confidence.

  • Deterministic ID: SKILL-MCH-0482 represents Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (TIG).
  • Auditability ("Show Me Why"): The original raw string, source URL, timestamp, and employer context are permanently linked to the canonical binding.
  • NSQF Aligned: Bound to National Skills Qualification Framework QP/NOS standards.
  • Multi-Dimensional Vectors: 768-dimensional semantic embeddings capture cross-domain proximity.

Confidence Scoring & Disambiguation

Mathematical formula balancing lexical overlap, semantic proximity, and source verification weights

Scoring Formulation

Every canonical association is assigned a rigorous composite score between 0.00 and 1.00:

Confidence = (w₁ · Sim_lexical) + (w₂ · Sim_semantic) + (w₃ · Ctx_cooccurrence) + (w₄ · Rel_source)
  • w₁ (0.25): Jaro-Winkler string similarity & phonetic edit distance.
  • w₂ (0.35): Cosine similarity between 768-d domain vector embeddings.
  • w₃ (0.25): Bayesian co-occurrence likelihood within the posting's designated sector.
  • w₄ (0.15): Verified reliability coefficient of the reporting source.

Action Threshold Rules

How the SkillPulse decision engine routes signals based on their computed confidence score:
